Privacy Policy

DRAFT UPDATED SEPTEMBER 19, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Orbit Relay. The game does not require an account and its core gameplay works offline.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked sectors, scores, and shard totals are stored locally on your device. Orbit Relay does not operate a cloud account system and does not upload this local game progress to the developer.

Advertising

Orbit Relay uses TopOn to mediate rewarded video and interstitial advertising. Depending on which ad is served, TopOn and an advertising partner may process technical and advertising information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app and ad placement identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention data.

The mediation configuration may include AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Chartboost, Digital Turbine, InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff Monetize, Mintegral, Pangle, Unity Ads, and Kwai. These companies process information under their own privacy policies. The active partner for a particular ad may vary by region, availability, and auction result.

Your choices

On supported Apple devices, the system may ask whether you allow tracking. If you decline, Orbit Relay does not receive access to the device advertising identifier and configures TopOn for non-personalized advertising. Declining tracking does not remove game features or rewards. You may change the system choice later in iOS Settings.

Rewarded video is optional. Interstitial advertising is shown only at selected breaks between rounds and is frequency-capped.

Why information is used

Advertising partners may use the information described above to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, maintain service reliability, and—only where permitted—personalize advertising.

Sharing and international processing

Advertising information may be shared among TopOn, the ad partner that serves an ad, measurement providers, service providers, and legal authorities when required. These providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live and apply their own retention periods and safeguards.

Children

Orbit Relay is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising partner, please use the contact path below so the request can be routed appropriately.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or to object to certain advertising uses. Requests concerning data controlled by an advertising partner may need to be made directly to that partner. We will help identify the appropriate route when possible.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact shown on the Orbit Relay App Store product page and include “Orbit Relay Privacy” in the subject. You can also review current troubleshooting and contact guidance on the Support page.
