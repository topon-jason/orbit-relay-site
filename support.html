PLAYER SUPPORT

Get your signal moving again.

Orbit Relay is designed to work offline. Advertising rewards need a network connection, but a missing ad never blocks normal play.

An ad is not ready

Check your connection, wait a moment, and try again. Ad availability varies by region. You can always continue without the optional reward.

A reward was not added

The reward is granted only after the ad provider reports completion. If an ad closes early or loses connection, your run and standard reward remain unchanged.

The game paused

Orbit Relay pauses when iOS sends the app to the background. Return to the game and tap Resume to continue the same sector.

Progress appears reset

Progress is stored only on the device. Removing the app, clearing its data, or restoring without app data may remove saved sectors and scores.

Change tracking permission

Open iOS Settings, choose Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Game access and rewards never depend on enabling tracking.

Report an ad

Use the information or privacy control shown inside the ad when available. Include a screenshot, approximate time, country, and device model in a support request.

Contact

Use the Contact Developer address on the Orbit Relay App Store product page. Include the app version, iOS version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or full advertising identifiers.
